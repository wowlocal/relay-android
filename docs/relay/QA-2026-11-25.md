## Worked well

- **Login:** a bad address ("Invalid Address"), an unreachable server ("Can't Connect"), a non-Mattermost host ("Not a Mattermost Server"), a wrong password ("Incorrect Login"), MFA (wrong code → "Incorrect Code", right code → chat list), and the notification permission prompt after login (`qa-02`…`qa-10`).
- **First sync:** the chat list was ready within ~5 s of the MFA code. Team folders, unread counts (All 14 / Design 9 / Engineering 8) and mention badges match REST. Custom status emoji shows by the title.
- **Messages:** markdown renders right after sending (bold, italic, code, strike, emoji, link), and the server gets the markdown. Edit ("edited"), delete for everyone, 👍 from the quick reaction bar (server `+1`), swipe-to-reply (DM `root_id`), forward (permalink post, "Forwarded from Bob Brennan"), pin + pinned bar, and Copy Link (`/engineering/pl/<id>`) all work.
- **Live updates from bob (REST):** new post, edit, ❤️ reaction and delete appear within ~2 s.
- **Threads:** the "3 Replies" footer opens the thread, a reply goes in with the right `root_id`, and live replies land in the open thread.
- **Media:** a 2-photo album with caption (one post, 2 files), video, PDF and a voice message (`relay_voice` props) all send. Received photo and video render. The viewer opens, Save to gallery works, Share out works, and share-in to Saved Messages works (L1).
- **Notifications:** Reply from a notification posts in the DM. Tapping an expanded DM notification opens that chat. DND (carol's original status) suppresses notifications, as on iOS.
- **Offline:** "Waiting for network…" shows in airplane mode. Two messages sent offline show clocks. They survive `am force-stop` and go out once, in order, after reconnecting.
- **Other:** in-chat search with "1 of 2" navigation, jump to date, channel creation (team/name/URL), status menu → REST, passcode lock/unlock, logout (server session revoked, notifications cleared) and re-login, landscape, dark mode, Storage Usage.
- **Firehose (11,726 posts):** 25 flings loaded 14 pages of 50 at 50–150 ms per page. Emulator frame stats: 734 frames, 10.6 % janky, p50 26 ms, p90 53 ms.
